About

Gemma

Founder · MScPT student, UBC

  • BSc Kinesiology
  • MScPT Student, UBC
  • Based in Vancouver

I started PhysioReady because I wished I'd had it.

When I started applying to physiotherapy school, I felt completely lost. The information was scattered, the timelines were confusing, and I didn't know a single person who had been through it.

I'm a Kinesiology grad and a current first-year MScPT student at UBC. I applied to multiple Canadian physiotherapy programs across the country, received interview invitations from several of the most competitive ones, and was accepted on my first cycle.

I'm not sharing that to impress you. I'm sharing it because I remember exactly how overwhelming this felt, and I know how much a steady, honest voice would have changed the experience. That's the support I'm trying to be for the next round of applicants.

PhysioReady is small and intentionally so. Every student I work with gets real attention, real feedback, and a mentor who actually picks up the phone.

Who it's for

Wherever you are in the process, you're not behind.

Applying to physio school is a multi-year process. Here's a realistic look at the timeline, and where mentorship fits at each stage.

  1. Year 1–2
    Build prerequisites & meaningful experience
  2. Summer
    Draft personal statement & school list
  3. Sep–Dec
    Submit applications across programs
  4. Jan–Mar
    Interview invites & MMI prep
  5. Apr–May
    Decisions land
Stage one

Early undergrad, mapping it out.

You know you want this, but the prerequisites, volunteer hours, and timelines feel like a maze. Let's build a plan you can actually follow.

Stage two

Final year, applying this cycle.

You're deep in personal statements and program research and the deadlines feel close. We'll move through it together, calmly and on time.

Stage three

Reapplicant, going again.

One cycle didn't go the way you hoped. We'll look honestly at what to strengthen, and build an application that reflects how much you've grown.
